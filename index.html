    About binaural beats & the Monroe Institute

    When two pure tones of slightly different frequencies are played one to each ear, the brain perceives a third “beat” equal to their difference. This can encourage a frequency-following response and inter-hemispheric synchronization — the principle behind Robert Monroe’s Hemi-Sync® research and the Focus levels (Focus 10, 12, 15, 21…). This tool uses only pure sine waves via the Web Audio API.
